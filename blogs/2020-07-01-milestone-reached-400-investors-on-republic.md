For hundreds of years, the opportunity to invest in a private company was limited by either who you knew or how much money you made. If you weren’t an accredited investor or a friend/family member of the founding team — there was simply no option for you to participate. Today, we live in an age where crowdfunding has democratized the ability for anyone to own an equity stake in the next unicorn 🦄.
