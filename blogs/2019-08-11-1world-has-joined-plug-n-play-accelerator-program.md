By 1World_Online via Medium 1World Online has been recently selected through a rigorous process to take part in Plug-n-Play’s Silicon Valley Program! From over 1,000 startups, 100 were shortlisted. From there the list was narrowed down to 30 via receiving feedback from PnP Corporate Partners, and then again to 15, who were selected during the Final Pitch to participate in the program.
